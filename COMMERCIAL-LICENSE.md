# Commercial License

Developer Control Plane is also available under a commercial license
for customers who cannot use the AGPL-3.0 open-source release.

## Why a commercial option exists

Developer Control Plane is open-source under AGPL-3.0. That license
protects user freedom for public-facing deployments, but it is not
suitable for every business. This commercial option preserves the
open-source project while giving organizations a straightforward path
to production use under conventional commercial terms.

## What the commercial license covers

- Use of Developer Control Plane without AGPL copyleft obligations
- Deployment in internal tools, SaaS offerings, or managed services
- Redistribution with proprietary modules or closed integrations
- Priority support and maintenance options
- Custom feature development and roadmap alignment

## When to choose the commercial license

- Your organization cannot share modified source code with users or
  clients under network-interaction requirements
- You are embedding Developer Control Plane into a proprietary product
  or hosted service with closed components
- You need indemnification, support SLAs, or enterprise terms
