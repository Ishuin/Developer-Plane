# Revenue Roadmap: $1,000+ from Developer Control Plane

## Revenue Model
**Open Core SaaS** with local-first free tier

### Revenue Streams
1. **Pro Subscription**: $15/month per user
2. **Team Seats**: $30/month per seat (2-10 users)
3. **Enterprise Licenses**: $500-2000/seat/year
4. **Support Contracts**: $500-2000/month
5. **Managed Deployment**: one-time + monthly fee

## Path to $1,000

### Month 1-2: Foundation
- Launch open source on GitHub
- Build initial community (target: 200 stars, 100 Discord members)
- No revenue yet, focus on product-market fit signals

### Month 3: First Revenue
- Launch Pro tier at $15/month
- Target: 5-10 paying customers
- Revenue: $75-150/month
- Convert 5 "founding users" from community

### Month 4-5: Growth
- Launch team tier
- Target: 15-20 paying customers total
- Revenue: $300-600/month
- Add Stripe billing, usage dashboard

### Month 6: Scale
- Target: 25-35 paying customers
- Revenue: $500-1000/month
- Launch Product Hunt campaign
- First enterprise inquiry

### Month 7-8: $1,000+ Milestone
- Target: 35-50 paying customers
- Revenue: $800-1500/month
- Mix: 30 solo + 5 team + 1 enterprise
- Profitability: mostly margin (cloud costs minimal)

## Customer Acquisition Math

### To Reach $1,000/Month at $15/Solo + $30/Team
- 40 solo + 3 team (6 seats) = $600 + $180 = $780
- 30 solo + 5 team (10 seats) = $450 + $300 = $750
- 20 solo + 1 enterprise (5 seats @ $50/seat) = $300 + $250 = $550
- **Realistic mix**: 25 solo + 4 team (8 seats) = $375 + $240 = $615
- **Enterprise deal**: 1 @ $2,000/year = $167/month
- **Target**: 25 solo + 4 team + 1 enterprise = $782/month
- **Need**: 10 more solo or 1-2 more team deals = $1,000/month

### Conversion Rate Assumptions
- GitHub stars to signups: 1-2%
- Signups to active use: 30%
- Active users to paid: 5-10%
- Needed signups: 200-400
- Needed visitors: 10,000-20,000

## Accelerators

### Fastest Path to $1k
1. **Enterprise deal** (one $2,000-5,000 contract)
2. **Sponsorship** (dev tool companies sponsor open source project)
3. **Consulting** (help teams adopt, $150-300/hour)
4. **Training** (workshops on AI agent orchestration, $500-1000/session)
5. **Affiliate** (refer Claude Code, Codex, Kimi subscriptions)

### Revenue Diversification
- Consulting: 20% of revenue
- Training: 15% of revenue
- Subscriptions: 50% of revenue
- Sponsorships: 10% of revenue
- Enterprise: 5% of revenue
